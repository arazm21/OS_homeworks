1.we see that only the first four and last 3 pages are used in 
the page table. everything else is empty, not dirty or anything, 
not touched. the last 3 pages are 
0x213 - user,valid, readable - could be a guard page.
0x2c7 - valid readable writable accessed dirty - trapframe, as those
are the permition bits of trapframes.
0x24b - valid, readable, executable, accessed - this is the trampoline
to communicate with the kernel, rx are the trampoline bits.
0x0-0x4000 - user space memory, the one at the beggining is r,u AND x,
so it could be user text, user data after that, and stack and heap after those,
as they all have VRW bit active.
